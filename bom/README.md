# Bill of materials

The owner-authorized Phase 3 motion-class review BOM is
[phase-3-motion-bom.md](phase-3-motion-bom.md). It is sample-characterization
guidance only, not a production purchasing release.

The Phase 3A packaging sample boundary is
[phase-3a-packaging-study.md](phase-3a-packaging-study.md). It covers only
measurements and coupons needed to review compact packaging; it is not a Phase
4 production BOM.

The Phase 4 preliminary structural BOM boundary is
[phase-4-preliminary-structural-bom.md](phase-4-preliminary-structural-bom.md).
It separates safe sample/coupon purchases from items that must wait for actual
hardware measurements, physical evidence, or owner review. It is not a
production BOM. Every future production line should include quantity,
description, status, source or specification, and the decision record that
justifies it.

The active transition is the [hardware procurement / measurement
matrix](hardware-procurement-measurement-matrix.md), backed by the
[requirements freeze](../requirements/hardware-procurement-measurement-freeze.md).
It is the controlling A-D list for characterization purchases and owned
hardware identification. EDR-014 authorizes the historical two-part Phase 5
candidate sub-gate; EDR-016 authorizes the master-assembly-first virtual-machine
review but does not authorize a production BOM, purchase order, or
manufacturing release.
The complete virtual-machine review BOM is
[phase5-complete-machine-bom.md](phase5-complete-machine-bom.md). It retains
the owner-supplied Arduino Mega + CNC Shield and owner NEMA17 stock as
`OWNER-SUPPLIED — DO NOT BUY`, while all unmeasured motion, spindle,
fastener, insert, and controller interfaces remain `BUY AFTER MEASUREMENT` or
provisional.
