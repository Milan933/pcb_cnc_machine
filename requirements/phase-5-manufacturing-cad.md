# Phase 5 - first manufacturing-CAD batch (historical sub-gate)

**Status:** historical first-base-pair sub-gate; superseded for active scope by
[phase-5-complete-machine.md](phase-5-complete-machine.md)
**Decision record:** [EDR-014](../docs/decisions/014-phase-5-base-pair-manufacturing-cad.md)
**Current maturity target:** `PROTOTYPE-STL`

The owner has opened Phase 5 from baseline
`afe2e14089467321b323d74f928a7ab4c5ffdc1f`. This phase converts the accepted
Phase 4A O2 structural architecture from review compounds into actual
parametric, printable PETG geometry. The first batch is deliberately limited
to:

- `base_left_integrated`
- `base_right_integrated`

The remaining 17 O2 parts were initially held at this sub-gate. The owner’s
later explicit Phase 5 authorization supersedes that stop and is recorded in
EDR-016; the complete-machine requirements now control active work.

## Manufacturing-CAD requirements

The base pair must be genuine fused solids, not review blocks or an unfused
compound. Each part must contain, in a documented local print frame:

- closed front/rear and side perimeter walls;
- a continuous load-path web between the perimeter and the Y rail carrier;
- repeated transverse ribs that divide and stiffen the carrier region;
- a rail support pad and positive printed shoulder, while treating raw PETG
  as a datum that still requires inspection, skim, or shim;
- a center-tie shear land and serviceable structural openings;
- clearly labeled provisional fastener/rail interfaces where the purchased
  hardware has not been measured.

The geometry is generated by
[`cad/parts/phase5_structural.py`](../cad/parts/phase5_structural.py). Its
dimensions are a part-specific parametric contract, traceable to the accepted
O2 envelope and the Phase 5 review boundary. The generator must preserve
deterministic names, local coordinates, and a reproducible parameter snapshot.

## Hardware boundary

The controller remains owner-supplied **Arduino Mega + CNC Shield**. The exact
Shield revision, installed driver modules, microstep/current/voltage/cooling
capability, I/O, and GRBL-compatible configuration remain identification and
commissioning items. No controller replacement is authorized by assumption.

The motors remain owner-supplied **NEMA17 stock — do not buy**. The base pair
does not depend on a final motor selection. Later motor mounts shall use the
generic 42.3 mm mounting interface, screening 5 mm shaft, 40-48 mm common body
envelope, and rear connector/wiring access. Exact motor characterization and
axis assignment remain open.

Rail, fastener, foot, and center-tie dimensions used by the first base pair
are explicitly `PROVISIONAL_HARDWARE_DIMENSION`. They are suitable for a
test-printable candidate, not for a fit, tolerance, or production claim.

## Print and export contract

- Print orientation is flat on the local XY base face, with Z upward.
- The 300 mm length is at the accepted preferred printer boundary and must be
  reviewed for warp, brim/skirt choice, first-layer behavior, and long-axis
  dimensional drift.
- The CAD source must produce one local STEP and one local STL per base part.
- Candidate outputs belong under ignored
  `generated/step/phase5-base-pair/` and
  `generated/stl/phase5-base-pair/`; they must not be placed in `release/`.
- The generator writes a reproducible manifest containing the source revision,
  parameter snapshot, bounding boxes, maturity, unresolved interfaces, and
  validation results.

## Validation and maturity

The first batch is accepted for owner review only when automated checks confirm:

1. both named parts exist;
2. each part is a valid single fused solid;
3. each part matches the declared print envelope and the conservative 320 mm
   XY bound;
4. both STEP and STL derivatives exist and are non-empty; and
5. the candidate remains labeled `PROTOTYPE-STL` with no release claim.

The candidate validation may pass geometry/export checks while reporting
`NOT_READY` warnings for provisional hardware interfaces and release status.
It must fail closed on invalid geometry, missing parts, unexpected solid
fragmentation, oversized prints, or missing exports.

## Physical first-print review

The owner review stop requires the two STL files to be opened in OrcaSlicer and
checked for walls, ribs, rail pad/shoulder, openings, support needs, brim, long
axis warping risk, and estimated print time/material. Before any fit claim,
the first PETG prints must be dimensionally inspected and compared with
representative Y rails, fasteners, feet, and the center tie. Rail datum
parallelism, post-processing, insert/fastener strategy, and service access must
be recorded as evidence rather than inferred from the mesh.

The first-print review did not authorize the remaining structural parts under
the original sub-gate. EDR-016 now authorizes the complete master-assembly
candidate pass, while physical evidence, measured interfaces, and release
remain open.
