# Hardware-library third-party notice

The library currently contains no downloaded third-party STEP/STP files.
All committed geometry is project-generated parametric reference, interface,
or envelope geometry. It is not an assertion that the represented hardware is
owner-measured or manufacturing-validated.

The following public sources are attributed in the manifest and are used only
for dimensional/provenance reference:

- HIWIN MG-series dimensional catalog for MGN9/MGN12 guide classes;
- Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3 open-hardware documentation;
- SKF 608-class bearing dimensions;
- MISUMI 5 x 8 coupling family information;
- SycoTec 5045 AC-ER11 product and CAD-download page;
- Omron D2F product and dimensional documentation.

Manufacturer and distributor CAD download terms remain separate from the
project's own source license. Do not add a vendor CAD file here unless its
redistribution permission is clear and the manifest records the exact source
filename, checksum, attribution, and license text or notice required by that
permission.
