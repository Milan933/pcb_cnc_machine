# Generic mechanical CAD research basis

Retrieved 2026-10-07. These sources were used to synthesize the skills; the skill text is original guidance, not copied source text.

| Topic | Source | Applied principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Systems engineering, reviews, verification and validation | [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf) and [NASA handbook appendix](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/system-engineering-handbook-appendix/) | Plan verification and validation from requirements, use phase reviews and configuration control, and treat analysis, inspection, demonstration, test, fit-check, and physical models as distinct evidence types. |
| Top-down/master-model design | [Autodesk Top-Down Design](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2023/ENU/Inventor-Help/files/GUID-3EDEE28E-2884-4507-8DA7-0AE52977A5ED.htm) and [Autodesk assembly workflows](https://help.autodesk.com/view/fusion360/ENU/?contextId=ASM-OVERVIEW) | Use a layout or skeleton to control shared relationships; combine top-down and bottom-up methods deliberately instead of forcing one method on every component. |
| Assembly constraints and DOF | [Onshape Mates](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Assembly/mates.htm) and [Onshape assemblies](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Assembly/assembly.htm) | Define relationships by intended movement, use explicit local mate/reference frames, and distinguish a fixed component from a constrained moving component. |
| Datums and geometric specification | [ISO 1101:2017](https://www.iso.org/cms/live/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/06/67/66777.html?browse=tc) and [Onshape Datum](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Drawing/datum.htm) | Use functional datum systems and communicate geometric variation rather than relying on unqualified nominal dimensions. |
| FDM orientation, walls, fit, and manifolds | [Prusa: Modeling with 3D printing in mind](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/modeling-with-3d-printing-in-mind_164135) | Account for anisotropy, warping/shrinkage, fit clearance, orientation, surface quality, and manifold solids; parametric iteration and test prints are expected. |
| FDM support and segmentation | [Prusa: Support material](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/support-material_1698?product=cw1) | Change orientation or split a part when that improves support removal and print reliability; do not make an impossible one-piece print sacred. |
| Heat-set inserts and polymer joints | [SPIROL Inserts for Plastics Design Guide](https://www.spirol.com/assets/files/ins-threaded-inserts-design-guide-us.pdf), [SPIROL insert-hole guidance](https://my.spirol.com/resources/white-papers/how-to-design-the-proper-hole-for-heat-ultrasonic-inserts/), and [SPIROL 3D-printing insert guidance](https://www.spirol.com/resources/white-papers/how-to-select-a-threaded-insert-for-your-3d-printed-assembly/) | Insert performance depends on hole size, depth, installation, boss/wall material, mating clearance, layer/process details, and testing; nominal thread size is not enough. |
| Linear guides and preload | [THK rigidity and preload guidance](https://www.thk.com/eu/en/products/lm_guide/selection/0008/) and [THK mounting procedure](https://www.thk.com/eu/en/products/lm_guide/maintenance/) | Guide stiffness, moment loading, preload, mounting surfaces, tightening, and measurement after installation are part of guide integration. |
| Locating/non-locating bearing arrangements | [SKF bearing combinations](https://cdn.skfmediahub.skf.com/api/public/0901d196802809de/pdf_preview_medium/0901d196802809de_pdf_preview_medium.pdf) and [SKF bearing application principles](https://cdn.skfmediahub.skf.com/api/public/0901d19680495562/pdf_preview_medium/0901d19680495562_pdf_preview_medium.pdf) | One support normally establishes axial location while another accommodates displacement; both radial and axial load paths and retention must be designed. |
| Shaft couplings and misalignment | [Lovejoy engineering data](https://www.lovejoy-inc.com/products/engineering-data/) and [Lovejoy coupling handbook](https://www.lovejoy-inc.com/resources/the-lovejoy-coupling-handbook/) | Select for torque, speed, shaft sizes, axial travel, angular/parallel misalignment, torsional behavior, service factor, and reaction loads; alignment remains a design and installation requirement. |
| Actuator selection | [Oriental Motor speed-torque curves](https://www.orientalmotor.com/stepper-motors/technology/speed-torque-curves-for-stepping-motors.html) | Select actuators from torque/force at operating speed with the actual driver, voltage, acceleration, inertia, and margin; holding torque alone is insufficient. |
| build123d coordinate and export behavior | [build123d key concepts](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/stable/key_concepts.html), [builder mode](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/key_concepts_builder.html), [design tutorial](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorial_design.html), and [import/export](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/stable/import_export.html) | Use explicit local planes and locations, deterministic transforms, functional origins, reusable builders, and explicit STEP/STL export settings while keeping the methodology backend-independent. |

The sources support principles, not automatic design approval. The agent must still identify application-specific loads, materials, tolerances, supplier data, and physical evidence.
