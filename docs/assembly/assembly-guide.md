# PCB CNC complete virtual assembly guide

Status: ordered assembly plan for the Phase 5 virtual machine; not a claim
that the printed candidates have passed physical fit or process validation.

The source assembly is generated by
`cad/assembly/phase5_complete_assembly.py`. The printable inventory is
`PCNC-P001` through `PCNC-P019`. Use the local STL/STEP derivatives under
`generated/stl/phase5-complete-machine/` and
`generated/step/phase5-complete-machine/` only as review candidates.

## Before assembly

- Read [coordinate-system.md](coordinate-system.md), the fastener schedule,
  and the unresolved-interface list in the complete-machine review.
- Condition PETG parts in the same environment in which they will be
  measured. Inspect warping, split lines, closed sections, rail shoulders,
  bosses, and holes before installing hardware.
- Do not force a rail, bearing, insert, spindle, or motor into a printed
  opening. Measure the representative component and revise the provisional
  interface first.
- Have a machinist square, straightedge, dial indicator, calipers, feeler
  gauges, torque driver, soldering iron for inserts, and non-marring shims
  available.

## Ordered assembly sequence

1. Print and inspect PCNC-P001/P002 base modules. Confirm the local print
   frame and record actual dimensions before any rail fit.
2. Print PCNC-P003 and verify that the transverse tie lands seat against both
   base modules without forcing them inward.
3. Print PCNC-P007 through PCNC-P010 feet. Fit the intended rubber or metal
   support hardware only after measuring its fastener and washer stack.
4. Place the four feet on a stable table and adjust them until the support
   plane is level. Do not use PETG walls as leveling threads.
5. Place the left and right base modules on the feet. Keep the printed rail
   shoulders facing upward and the front service openings accessible.
6. Install the provisional base-to-foot fasteners finger-tight. Confirm that
   the base pair is not twisted before tightening in a diagonal sequence.
7. Install PCNC-P003 through the center tie lands. Use the documented M4
   structural class only after measuring the actual insert or through-bolt
   solution; the schedule lengths are provisional.
8. Measure the two Y rail seats. Select one as the master datum and record
   any shim or skim required.
9. Install the left and right MGN12-class Y rails. Align the master rail
   against its shoulder, then indicate the second rail parallel over its full
   supported length. Leave final torque until the bed can be checked.
10. Fit the Y fixed and floating bearing cartridges (PCNC-P005/P006). Put the
    axial bearing at the motor/fixed end and preserve axial float at the
    opposite end.
11. Fit the PCNC-P004 Y motor service pocket. Confirm that the owner-stock
    NEMA17 shaft and connector can be installed and removed without removing
    the base.
12. Install the measured T8x4 Y screw, nut, coupler, and owner-stock motor.
    Check that the screw is straight, the coupler is not axially loaded, and
    the floating bearing remains free.
13. Install the four MGN12H Y carriages. Verify smooth travel by hand before
    attaching the bed frame.
14. Print and inspect PCNC-P019. Measure its four carriage pad locations and
    the centered nut-boss opening against the actual carriage and nut.
15. Attach the moving bed frame to the four Y carriages. Fit the Y nut with
    the screw centered; the fasteners must clamp the nut, not pull the frame
    out of plane.
16. Install the 230 x 180 x 12 mm spoilboard as a replaceable surface. Leave
    enough access for removal and allow a skim or shim operation.
17. Install provisional PCB registration and clamp hardware. Confirm that a
    200 x 150 mm board remains fully usable with the probe parked clear.
18. Print and inspect PCNC-P012/P013 gantry modules. Confirm that the J1
    tongue/socket and base shoulders clean up without changing the intended
    beam height.
19. Install the left and right gantry modules to their base datums. Square
    each tower to the master Y rail before installing beam-clamp fasteners.
20. Insert the J1 beam tongue into the socket. Clamp the joint with the
    measured insert/fastener strategy; printed shoulders and the tongue carry
    shear while fasteners supply preload.
21. Install the X lower MGN12 rail against its printed shoulder. Set the
    upper rail from the lower rail using the measured 60 mm center spacing,
    not an unmeasured printed face.
22. Install the X fixed and floating bearing cartridges (PCNC-P014/P015),
    then the measured T8x4 screw and coupler. Keep the fixed bearing at the
    drive end and preserve axial float at the far end.
23. Install the four X carriages and the X/Z backbone (PCNC-P016). Move the
    backbone through the full X screening travel before final rail torque.
24. Install the dual MGN9 Z rails to the backbone. Match the actual carriage
    spacing and shim the printed seats as required.
25. Install the Z screw, nut, fixed support, coupler, and the strongest
    electrically compatible owner-stock NEMA17 candidate only for a
    characterization fit. Final motor assignment remains open.
26. Install the Z carriage plate (PCNC-P017). Check that the nut is centered,
    the rails remain free, and the service side of the coupler is accessible.
27. Install the modular spindle mount (PCNC-P018). Set its centerline from
    the measured spindle envelope; do not assume the 52 mm screening bore is
    a final spindle bore.
28. Fit the selected spindle only after recording diameter, mass, cable exit,
    thermal clearance, ER11/tooling state, and runout. Keep the tool envelope
    clear of the bed except where cutting access is intended.
29. Install X, Y, and Z home-limit brackets and the conductive probe bracket.
    Confirm switch actuation direction and probe access at both work-area
    edges.
30. Install the electronics rail and mount the owner Arduino Mega + CNC
    Shield platform. Leave the exact driver module and shield I/O mapping
    provisional until the hardware is identified.
31. Route motor, limit, probe, and spindle-control cables through the modeled
    service zones. Provide strain relief and moving loops; never let a cable
    become a guide or screw stop.
32. Perform the alignment procedures below with fasteners only hand-tight
    until each datum is confirmed. Tighten the final schedule sequentially.
33. Run the full travel by hand with power removed. Check X/Y/Z extremes,
    spindle-to-gantry clearance, bed-to-tower clearance, screw/coupler access,
    and cable slack.
34. After electrical identification and a dry run, home each axis at low
    speed, check direction and limit behavior, and record the actual firmware
    configuration. Do not infer GRBL pins from a generic shield image.
35. Fit a sacrificial tool and perform spindle tram, PCB registration, and
    probing checks before any copper test cut.

## Alignment and service procedures

- **Y rails:** master one rail against its shoulder; indicate the second rail
  parallel over the full 310 mm screening length. Shim under the rail rather
  than bending PETG with fastener torque.
- **X rails:** align the lower rail first, then set the upper rail to the
  measured carriage spacing. Verify both rail blocks move without binding at
  both bearing ends.
- **Z rails:** use a gauge or the actual MGN9 carriage spacing. Keep the
  spindle centerline close to the guide plane and verify that the screw nut
  does not side-load the carriage.
- **Gantry squareness:** indicate each tower against the master Y rail, then
  check the X beam is square to Y at both ends. Recheck after PETG has
  thermally conditioned.
- **Bed:** indicate the bed frame to the Y carriage plane, then shim or skim
  the replaceable spoilboard. Do not use the PCB itself as a structural datum.
- **Spindle tram:** use a known straight test bar or a repeatable cylindrical
  artifact. Tram to the spoilboard after the bed has been leveled and before
  probing.
- **Service access:** motors, couplers, bearing cartridges, screws, limits,
  probe, controller, shield, and spoilboard must be removable without
  dismantling the primary fixed force loop.

## Final tightening and evidence

Record the actual fastener, insert, rail, motor, spindle, and controller data
in the hardware identification sheets. Record torque only after the fastener
and insert manufacturer data are known. Complete the first-print inspection,
alignment record, electrical pinout record, and full-travel service check
before changing any part to `HARDWARE-VALIDATED` or `RELEASED`.
