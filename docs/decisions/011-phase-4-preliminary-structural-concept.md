## Preliminary structural architecture baseline

Authorize the preliminary structural concept for the accepted P2 fixed-gantry/
moving-Y-bed baseline. The concept uses printed PETG load paths and a modular
decomposition whose mandatory parts remain at or below the conservative 320 mm
XY build-plate boundary, with the preferred 300 mm boundary used where
practical.

The concept contains 29 named structural parts:

- a segmented closed-section base perimeter, side members, rail carriers,
  center tie, feet, and service cartridges;
- two hollow gantry towers and two split X torsion-box beam segments;
- replaceable X bearing cartridges;
- an X/Z backplate, Z carriage, Z bearing and motor service cartridges, and a
  parametric 52 mm-screened spindle mount;
- a ribbed 230 x 180 mm moving bed with a centered-nut service boss.

The P2 motion and hardware envelopes are included as references, not as
printed geometry. The review assembly is generated by
`cad/assembly/phase4_assembly.py`; the temporary review runner is
`tools/run_phase4_preliminary_study.py`.

The detailed [Phase 4 owner-review package](../architecture/phase-4-owner-review-package.md)
contains the 29-part inventory, mass and joint audit, force-loop trace,
deflection breakdown, simplification candidates, dimensional/hardware
summaries, and A-J review views.

## Structural and interface decisions

1. The base uses separated front/rear perimeter segments, closed side members,
   dual Y rail carriers, a centered transverse tie with a Y-nut relief, and
   replaceable motor/bearing cartridges. It avoids a large flat printed plate.
2. The fixed gantry uses two broad hollow towers and a split 90 mm deep X
   torsion-box concept. The beam is split at the center because a mandatory
   one-piece 330 mm-class print is not accepted without printer-specific proof.
3. J1, a deep tongue-and-groove/socket joint, is selected provisionally over
   J2 stepped keyed shoulder and J3 interlocking rib/shear-key. The tongue,
   socket, shoulders, and mating faces carry location and shear; M4 inserts
   provide clamp preload. J1 is not production-frozen.
4. X rail seats are broad pads integrated into the torsion-box concept; Z rail
   seats are ribs on the X/Z backplate; Y rail seats are thick base carriers.
   A printed shoulder, replaceable shim/reference strip, measurement, and
   skimming strategy are required. Raw PETG is not treated as a precision
   rail datum.
5. The moving bed is a low-mass perimeter/rib structure with a replaceable
   spoilboard and serviceable centered Y nut. The bed throat and tower spacing
   are checked against the actual 230 mm bed width.
6. All motion hardware, spindle, limits, probe/wiring, and spoilboard remain
   removable in the concept sequence.

## Alternatives considered

### One-piece 330 mm torsion box

Rejected for this Phase 4 concept because the owner set a conservative 320 mm
maximum in either build-plate axis. It may be reconsidered only with measured
printer diagonal/usable-area evidence and a new owner decision.

### Unsplit 300 mm-class beam

Not selected because the fixed gantry's beam depth and rail-seat interfaces
make a single print unnecessarily dependent on printer conditioning and
service access. The split beam keeps each mandatory segment below the boundary
and exposes a measurable joint.

### J2 stepped shoulder and J3 interlocking ribs

Both remain credible fallbacks. J2 is simpler to print and inspect but less
captured against relative slip. J3 distributes reaction through several ribs
but adds interface count, insert locations, and assembly sensitivity. J1 has
the clearest geometry-first load path and is therefore provisional, not final.

### Solid plates or aluminum extrusion

Not selected. The project requires a predominantly printed PETG frame and
geometry-led stiffness. Metal remains allowed for rails, screws, bearings,
fasteners, inserts, shafts, couplers, and spindle where those are the
appropriate purchased components.

## Evidence and calculations

The preliminary equivalent-section screen uses 5 N, an effective PETG modulus
of 2000 N/mm², the 90 x 90 x 6 mm beam section, separate beam bending/torsion,
tower bending, J1 joint, X/Z, base, rail-seat, moving-bed, and asymmetric
racking contributions. The current calculated total is approximately 0.01265
mm, below the 0.020 mm target and 0.030 mm acceptance limit. It is not FEA,
not a strength calculation, and not measured PETG performance.

The pinned build123d review runner currently reports:

- 29 structural parts and 77 total assembly components;
- all mandatory print orientation XY extents at or below 300 mm, with the
  300 mm Y rail carriers explicitly at the preferred boundary;
- assembly bounds approximately 363 x 353 x 275 mm for the included geometry;
- zero unexpected solid interferences;
- non-empty temporary STEP/STL exports for the assembly, all structural parts,
  and J1/J2/J3 comparison geometry.

These are reproducibility results for the review model, not physical
acceptance.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Consequence | Mitigation / gate evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PETG creep or anisotropy reduces J1 preload or changes the rail datum. | Gantry slip, guide binding, or tool-point drift. | Print orientation, conditioned coupons, insert pull-out/torque/creep tests, and repeated service cycling. |
| A 300 mm Y rail carrier warps or cannot be skimmed/shimmed adequately. | Rail parallelism and moving-bed smoothness fail. | Printer-specific warp study, supported datum strips, measurement, and P1 fallback review. |
| Actual rail, screw, bearing, motor, or spindle dimensions exceed the screens. | Cartridges and clearances become invalid. | Measure hardware before manufacturing geometry or supplier-specific holes. |
| The provisional equivalent stiffness is optimistic. | 5 N tool-point target is missed. | Separate load-path test with beam/tower/joint/XZ/base/rail-seat/bed measurements; no FEA claim. |
| Service covers or insert tools are inaccessible after assembly. | Parts cannot be replaced without destructive disassembly. | Full-travel and service mock-up following the documented sequence. |
| Spindle thermal/cable envelope differs from the 52 mm screen. | Z force loop, clearance, or PETG temperature risk. | Identify and measure the spindle before freezing the mount. |

## BOM boundary

The preliminary BOM separates low-risk measurement/coupon purchases from items
that must wait for hardware measurement or owner review. Rails, carriages,
screws, nuts, bearings, couplers, fasteners, inserts, owned motors,
spoilboard, spindle, and electronics are listed without vendor dimensions.
Production quantities and supplier-specific printed interfaces are not
approved.
