## Decision proposed for owner review

Select O2, the balanced Phase 4A structural optimization, as the preliminary
candidate for the next owner review. O2 consolidates the primary printed
force loop while retaining serviceable motion, spindle, foot, electronics,
center-tie, and spoilboard interfaces.

O2 changes the review decomposition from 29 to 19 PETG parts and from 16 to
8 critical structural parts. Its controlled primary force-loop screen changes
from 17 to 9 PETG parts and from 17 to 7 pair-level structural joints. The
CAD solid-equivalent mass changes from 5.808293 kg to 4.720329 kg, while the
preliminary 5 N deflection screen changes from 0.012648 mm to 0.009410 mm.
The selected largest print is 150 x 300 x 40.75 mm.

This is a recommendation for owner review, not Phase 4 or Phase 4A
acceptance. No vendor-specific hardware, production insert geometry, release
STEP/STL, or Phase 5 work is authorized.

## Alternatives

### O1 conservative consolidation

O1 integrates the X/Z support only. It retains 27 parts, 16 critical parts,
17 primary-loop parts, and 17 primary pair-level joints. It is easy to
service and carries low alignment risk, but it does not simplify enough of
the base and gantry force loop.

### O2 balanced optimization

O2 integrates each base/Y/perimeter side, each tower/beam side, and the X/Z
support backbone. It retains removable rail, screw, bearing, motor, spindle,
foot, electronics, and center-tie interfaces. It reaches the preferred
deflection screen while keeping all XY prints at or below the 300 mm
preferred boundary.

### O3 aggressive integration

O3 integrates base, Y carrier, tower, beam, and X-bearing envelopes into two
large side modules and removes printed feet/electronics parts. It reaches
10 parts and a 0.008427 mm preliminary screen, but its 189 x 300 x 188 mm
side modules carry the highest conditioning, alignment, handling, repair, and
service risk. O3 is not selected.

## Evidence and validation

The implementation is generated by:

- cad/parts/phase4a_structural.py;
- cad/assembly/phase4a_assembly.py;
- cad/phase4a_calculations.py;
- cad/validation/phase4a.py;
- tools/run_phase4a_optimization.py; and
- tools/generate_phase4a_owner_review.py.

The O1/O2/O3 comparison and selected O2 review runner report:

- all three candidates at or below the 320 mm conservative XY bound;
- O2 at the 300 mm preferred boundary;
- P2 full-travel, swept-clearance, rail/screw, bed, spindle, motor, limit,
  and service-footprint checks retained;
- zero unexpected structural overlaps for every candidate;
- non-empty temporary STEP/STL exports for selected O2 parts, assembly, and
  J1/J2/J3 study;
- target, preferred, and acceptance deflection screens passing for all three
  candidates.

These are CAD/repository checks only. They do not prove PETG creep,
alignment, insert pull-out, service access, hardware fit, tool-point
deflection, or production printability.

## Open risks and required gates

1. J1 center beam split requires load, creep, preload-retention, and repeated
   disassembly evidence.
2. The 300 mm integrated base/Y rail datum requires conditioned printing,
   warp inspection, skim/shim, parallelism, and carriage-preload evidence.
3. The X/Z backbone requires actual rail, bearing, motor, screw, coupler,
   spindle, insert, and tool-access measurements.
4. The spindle centerline and thermal/cable envelope remain unresolved.
5. The moving-bed mass and complete moving assembly require measurement under
   PCB support and workholding loads.
6. Full-travel and service sequence mock-up remains required.

## Owner gate

**Proposed disposition:** require Phase 4A owner review of O2; do not accept
Phase 4 or Phase 4A yet.

Until the owner records a disposition, EDR-012 remains proposed, EDR-011
remains proposed, production release remains blocked, and Phase 5 remains
unstarted.
